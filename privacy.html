FitDay Privacy Policy

FitDay is a personal, single-user training companion app operated by its owner for their own use.

When connected to WHOOP, FitDay reads the owner's own recovery, sleep, and workout data via the official WHOOP API, solely to display it alongside the owner's training plan. Data is retrieved only for the account that authorized access.

FitDay does not collect data from anyone else, does not sell or share data with third parties, does not use advertising or analytics, and stores checklist state only in the browser's local storage on the owner's device.

To revoke access at any time, disconnect FitDay from your WHOOP account settings.
